Privacy Policy for Media Trk

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Media Trk ("the app") is a TV show tracking app for iOS. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

No account required

Media Trk does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no username, password, or personal profile associated with your use of the app.

Data stored on your device

The shows and episodes you track, along with your app preferences, are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us and we do not have access to it.

Third-party data source

Media Trk uses the TVmaze API to retrieve show and episode information. When you search for or track a show, requests are sent to TVmaze's servers to fetch that show's public data. No personal information about you is sent as part of these requests. Please refer to TVmaze's own policies for how they handle API request data.

Notifications

Media Trk may send local notifications (e.g. to alert you when a new episode of a tracked show is airing). These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and do not involve sending your data to a remote server.

Crash reports and diagnostics

Media Trk uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information so we can find and fix bugs. When the app crashes or encounters an error, information such as the crash log, device model, operating system version, app version, and the state of the app at the time of the crash may be sent to Google. Crashlytics also uses a randomly generated installation identifier to count unique crashes.

This information is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking you across other apps or websites, and is not used for advertising. See Google's Firebase privacy information for how Google handles this data.

Advertising

Media Trk displays ads through a third-party advertising network. This network may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier and general usage data in order to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by the advertising provider, not by us directly. You can manage ad tracking permissions through your device's Settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not sell any data to third parties
  • We do not link crash or diagnostic data to your identity

Children's privacy

Media Trk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to mymediatrk@gmail.com.

Media Trk uses the TVmaze API but is not affiliated with or endorsed by TVmaze.
